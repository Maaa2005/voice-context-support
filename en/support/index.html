Voice Context records and transcribes the conversations you have in person, then leaves them behind as decisions, action items, and background. Your conversations never pass through a server run by the developer — they stay between your device and the AI environment you set up yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Is the recorded audio saved?

No. Audio is processed for transcription and is never written to a file. What stays on your device is the transcript and the structured notes built from it.

Does the developer see my conversations?

No. There is no server operated by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details.

No summary or decisions are being generated

Structuring requires a destination. In Settings, configure one of the following.

  • Your Mac: start the Mac app, then paste the pairing URI it shows into the "pairing URI" field on your iPhone. Both devices must be on the same network.
  • A Gateway: enter the Gateway URL and token in Settings.

With neither configured, recording and transcription still work, but no structuring happens.

The app cannot reach my Mac

Check these in order.

  • Are the iPhone and the Mac on the same Wi-Fi network (not cellular)?
  • Is the Mac app running?
  • Is the Mac asleep?
  • Has Voice Context been granted the iOS "Local Network" permission (Settings > Voice Context)?

If it still fails, remove the pairing, issue a fresh pairing URI on the Mac, and pair again.

Recording will not start

In Settings > Voice Context, confirm that microphone and speech recognition permissions are enabled. Recording is also unavailable until you have read through the first-run explanation.

Can I hand over a conversation before it ends?

Yes. Tap "Send now" on the recording screen and everything said so far is structured while recording continues.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is available in English and Japanese, following your device's language setting.

Conversations are transcribed in Japanese.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual conversation records inside the app. Deleting the app from your device removes everything it has stored.

Before you start

  • Tell everyone present, in advance, that you are recording and that the transcript will be sent to an AI service. Complying with your organization's rules and your local laws is your responsibility.
  • Speech recognition produces errors, and summaries are generated by AI and may be inaccurate. Output cannot be used as a verbatim record or as evidence.
